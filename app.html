Deposit

A deposit names no market. It buys an unbet note you can stake in any market later, or withdraw without ever staking it — which is what makes the anonymity set every unspent note in the system rather than one market’s depositors.

Bet — which market

The market is chosen at BET time, not at deposit time. Pick one here, then use the Bet YES / Bet NO buttons on any unbet note below.

Your notes — this is the money

A note’s nullifier and secret are the only proof you own a position. There is no account and no recovery. Clearing browser data destroys them; export and keep them somewhere safe. The action button on each row is what that note is currently usable for — an unbet note can only bet, a position note can only redeem (once the market is resolved and settled), a settled note can only withdraw.

Withdrawing to your connected wallet re-links you to every bet you made. The recipient field defaults to a smaller amount and your own address — override both if you actually want privacy at exit:
  • Enter a fresh address you control to break the link. Fund its gas from a source that isn't already tied to you — never from the wallet that deposited.
  • Never consolidate several fresh withdrawal addresses back into one wallet; the fan-in is a stronger signal than the withdrawal ever was.
  • Smaller amounts over time look like every other deposit-sized rung. A full, lump-sum withdrawal is the easiest pattern to correlate.
