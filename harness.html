Atrum — browser proving harness

Measures what Groth16 proving actually costs in a browser, against the real circuits. Node timings are a lower bound; the browser multiplier is the number that decides the UX, because a user cannot place a first bet without downloading and proving bet_encrypted in this tab.

Each circuit is downloaded, proved once, and then verified — a fast wrong proof is worse than a slow right one. Inputs are the canned fixtures recorded by atrum-core’s proving spike, so this measures proving cost, not witness construction.
